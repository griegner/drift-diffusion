\section{Introduction}

binary decisions made upon the sequential accumulation of evidence\\
two-alternative forced-choice (2-AFC) tasks\\

observed decision speed and accuracy\\
unobserved decision making process (DMP) - latent in the sense that we do not observe it directly - underlying process that generates the observed data\\
approximated by drift diffusion model (DDM)\\
DMP $\ne$ DDM, to equate them requires many layers of approximation\\

DDM for describing how simple decisions are made in the brain - supported by arguments of neuro-plausibility from measuring the activity of ensembles of neurons - neural mechanisms underlying the decision process\\


observed decision speed and accuracy (behavioral observations) can also be explained by:
\begin{itemize}
    \item ex-Gaussian or shifted Wald distributions (Matzke and Wagenmakers, 2009)
    \item linear ballistic accumulator model (Brown and Heathcote)
    \item Leaky competing accumulator model (Usher and McClelland)
    \item Levy flight model (Wieschen et al 2020)
\end{itemize}

sequence of behavior:\\
iid assumption of consecutive decisions, a condition often violated in real data\\
account for trial-to-trial dependencies

parsimony\\
models should not only provide a good fit to data, but also be parsimonious in form and represent a clear mechanistic interpretation\\
